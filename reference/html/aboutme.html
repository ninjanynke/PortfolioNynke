About me

Hi! My name is Nynke.

I'm 27 years old and grew up in the Netherlands.

Over the recent years, I have completed a bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence (cum laude) and a master's degree in Interaction Technology (average of an 8/10). I have been working  as an Android Software Engineer and UX Designer since January 2020, after completing a UX Design internship in 2019.

But besides that collection of fancy words, I am mostly a curious person who loves creating things. Next to the digital realm in which I find myself at least 40 hours a week (plus some hours of Beat Saber in Virtual Reality), I'm a big fan of finding offline hobbies to occupy myself with: from sewing a Eurovision Song Contest outfit, creating clay earrings, painting thrifted furniture, to playing the accordeon - I wouldn't mind having a couple of extra hours each week...
