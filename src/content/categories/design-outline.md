Since I was 14 years old, I've been making digital creations. This went from virtually selling avatars and banners on an online horse game forum to creating a YouTube account to making posters for big parties and landing a UX Design internship in university. I love freely creating, but designing with boundaries (for apps/websites for example) is also a great challenge to take on. From ancient Paint, PhotoFiltre, GIMP, Sony Vegas Pro to the well known Adobe package, I've worked with many programs and it remains fun to explore and learn new programs.
