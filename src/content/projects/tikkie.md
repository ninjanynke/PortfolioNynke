In-between, as well as at the end of all expenses, users can settle up with each other and directly receive their money back through a Tikkie. A Tikkie is a quick payment request that has become an official word in the Dutch dictionary due to the app's popularity with over 8 million active users. Tikkie is officially created and owned by the ABN AMRO bank."
