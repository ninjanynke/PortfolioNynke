## Method

There's many different things one can do with macrame rope, but my favourite things remain the plant hangers. I've taking on other projects as well, such as creating a Christmas tree, a decorated, thrifted mirror and some custom hanging shelves. Macrame can be a very relaxing past-time, but I will never forget the projects where I ran out of rope, which is really hard to conceal or fix. So: always cut enough rope!
